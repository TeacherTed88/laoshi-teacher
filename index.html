语法 · GRAMMAR

不但……而且……Not only... but also...

Use this pattern to connect two ideas — the second one is even stronger than the first.

不但 + A ，而且 + B
他不但聪明，而且幽默。He's not only smart, but also humorous.
我不但喜欢音乐，而且喜欢唱歌。I don't just like music — I also like singing.
这门课不但有意思，而且很有用。This class is not only fun, but also very useful.

⚠️ Note: with verbs, often use 不但不/没 for negatives: 他不但不生气，而且很高兴。

对话 · DIALOGUE

对话：聊聊爱好Teacher 老师 & Student 学生 — listen and repeat!

师
你有什么爱好？ Nǐ yǒu shénme àihào?What hobbies do you have?
生
我特别喜欢打篮球，而且我坚持了很多年。 Wǒ tèbié xǐhuan dǎ lánqiú, érqiě wǒ jiānchíle hěn duō nián.I really like playing basketball, and I've kept it up for many years.
师
哇，那你打得怎么样？ Wa, nà nǐ dǎ de zěnmeyàng?Wow, how well do you play?
生
不但打得不错，而且我还是我们队的队长呢！
